Strategic brief · 6/30/2026

Creative brief

Create a premium cinematic brand awareness video ad for Kraken centered on a 24-hour journey across global cities, expressing that money is always moving and Kraken is present when people choose to act. The business goal is broad upper-funnel awareness for Kraken as a secure, global crypto platform.
Campaign objectives

Increase general brand awareness for Kraken among prospective crypto-curious and active trading audiences.

Reinforce Kraken's positioning as a secure, reliable platform available across time zones and global markets.

Create a premium, composed brand impression that supports trust and consideration.

Marketing strategy

Upper-funnel brand awareness creative designed to build trust and global relevance, with a cinematic narrative showing continuity of financial motion across cities and time zones.

KPIs

Brand lift / ad recall

Reach among target audience

Website visit lift

Demographics

Adults 25-44 in key global urban markets with disposable income and interest in finance, investing, technology, or crypto.

Psychographics

Globally minded, opportunity-seeking, digitally fluent, values control, security, transparency, and access to markets that move quickly.

Pain points

Concern about trusting crypto platforms, fear of missing opportunities across time zones, desire for a reliable platform that feels established and secure.

Preferences

Responds to premium, polished storytelling over hype; interested in finance, technology, and global culture; likely to engage with confident, composed messaging.

1. The World Never PausesSelected

AI-generated premium cinematic city montage

A premium look at global financial momentum that uses a seamless 24-hour city journey to show Kraken is ready whenever opportunity appears.

30sPremiumCinematicGlobal CitiesVoiceoverComposed Pace
Execution, rationale, visual direction

The film opens before sunrise over one city and glides, almost imperceptibly, into another as streets wake, exchanges glow, ferries move, screens reflect in glass, and the world’s rhythm begins to hum. With a calm, confident voiceover delivering lines like “Markets move. Cities move. Decisions move,” we travel through a composed chain of global moments—morning commutes, late-night office towers, airport corridors, rooftop horizons—each transition feeling less like a cut and more like money itself passing through time zones. The pacing stays smooth and deliberate, building trust rather than adrenaline, until the final sequence resolves into Kraken’s branded purple world and the line “When you choose to act, Kraken is ready,” followed by a restrained Learn More CTA.

This is the closest expression of the approved brief: premium, cinematic, global, and trust-first. Because surfaced assets are light on UI and heavy on atmospheric brand backdrops, an AI-generated city montage is a strong fit: it creates bespoke world-class urban imagery without depending on missing lifestyle footage or product screens. The route also allows highly controlled continuity across time zones, making the 24-hour concept feel elevated rather than generic stock.

Blue-hour to midnight city palettes with restrained Kraken purples woven into reflections and signage; elegant wides, controlled tracking shots, glass, steel, and atmospheric light; smooth transitions and low-friction motion energy.

2. The Moment You Move

AI-generated cinematic vignettes with human decision moments

A composed look at decisive action that uses intimate human moments across world cities to show Kraken is there when intention turns into movement.

30sPremiumHuman-CenteredCinematicTrustMeasured
Execution, rationale, visual direction

Rather than emphasizing the scale of cities first, this concept begins inside small, exact moments: a hand pausing over a phone at dawn, a trader looking out over a rain-lit skyline after hours, a traveler between flights as a distant city glows beyond the glass. Each vignette unfolds in a different part of the world, but all are tied together by the same emotional beat—the quiet second before someone decides to act. The voiceover is sparse and assured, saying only what matters: “Around the world, opportunity doesn’t announce itself. It arrives. And when it does, readiness matters.” As the sequence widens from intimate detail to urban scale, Kraken appears not as a loud disruptor but as the trusted presence behind decisive action, ending on logo, brand field, and Learn More.

This concept differentiates itself by making trust emotional and human rather than purely environmental. It still honors the brief’s global-city framework, but gives the audience relatable decision points that align with the target’s desire for control and confidence. AI generation is appropriate because it allows premium consistency across multiple international vignettes and precise cinematic blocking without requiring a costly multinational live-action shoot, while the limited brand asset set can still support end branding elegantly.

Close detail shots, reflective surfaces, weathered night textures, restrained lens movement, and soft practical light; city scale emerges gradually from intimate foreground moments; overall motion remains smooth, sparse, and composed.

3. Follow the Flow

Premium motion-graphics and AI city hybrid

A sophisticated look at global movement that uses elegant data-like motion through city imagery to show Kraken connects people to a world that never stops moving.

30sPremiumHybridMotion GraphicsGlobalOwnable Visual System
Execution, rationale, visual direction

The film starts with stillness: a dark city horizon at first light, interrupted by a single line of motion that travels like a pulse through streets, windows, tunnels, coastlines, and skylines. That line becomes the guiding visual language of the spot, sometimes feeling like market flow, sometimes like transit, sometimes like a personal choice rippling through the wider financial world. Clean supers and sparse voiceover do the strategic heavy lifting—“Across cities. Across hours. Across moments to act.”—while the motion system threads every scene together and gradually introduces Kraken purple as a mark of clarity and control. By the end, the abstract line resolves into the brand field and logo, making Kraken feel less like a trading app and more like a secure layer within global motion, with a simple Learn More close.

This concept introduces a materially different execution approach by blending cinematic city imagery with an elegant motion-graphics system, which is highly suitable for fintech positioning. The surfaced asset set includes atmospheric brand backdrops and a clean logo, supporting a premium branded graphic layer even without extensive UI assets. By making motion design the connective tissue rather than the subject, the concept stays cinematic and composed while giving Kraken a more ownable visual signature than a pure city montage.

Dark-to-luminous city photography with precise graphic linework, restrained typography, and selective Kraken purple accents; premium compositing, smooth camera drift, and moderate motion energy with high design control.

Script v1.0

The World Never Pauses

SCENE 01EXT. TOKYO SKYLINE / WATERFRONT / APARTMENT WINDOW - PRE-DAWN

A blue-hour view over Tokyo rests in near silence before sunrise. The skyline glows in scattered points, a ferry cuts softly across dark water, and reflections drift along glass towers. The mood remains premium, composed, and expansive, introducing the start of a deliberate 24-hour journey through three global financial cities.

The world moves.

Inside this opening city rhythm, YOUNG_PROFESSIONAL stands by a window as first light begins to gather beyond the glass. In one calm, understated moment, she checks Kraken on her phone before the day begins, the app glow reflected faintly in the window. Her movement is minimal and confident, suggesting readiness rather than urgency.

In Tokyo, it starts.

Tokyo begins to stir with restraint: transit lines glow, a few office windows brighten, and distant traffic traces a measured current through the frame. The character beat should feel naturally embedded in the city awakening, making Tokyo clearly the first handoff in a global chain of motion and decision.

SCENE 02EXT. LONDON FINANCIAL DISTRICT / TRANSIT / OFFICE THRESHOLDS - MORNING

Morning arrives in London as the film glides seamlessly out of Tokyo's blue hour into the bright precision of the City. Commuters move with purpose through a polished transit hub, light skims across steel and stone, and financial architecture carries the momentum forward without urgency.

Tokyo to London. Markets move. Cities move. Decisions move.

ENTREPRENEUR crosses between meetings through a refined office corridor and glass-lined threshold space. He pauses briefly in a pocket of quiet, monitors the market on Kraken, then continues forward with composed clarity. The beat should imply a global life in motion—disciplined, informed, and never frantic.

Elegant fragments of daily financial life unfold around him: a phone checked while waiting for a lift, sunlight climbing across office facades, measured foot traffic through a station. The pacing stays smooth and assured, preserving the trust-first tone while making London's role in the geographic progression explicit.

SCENE 03EXT. NEW YORK BUSINESS CENTERS / AIRPORT / WATERFRONT - DAY

The journey continues into New York at midday. Business towers, airport passageways, waterfront edges, and layered urban movement connect into the final city of the three-part chain. Light is crisp and controlled, with glass, steel, and long urban lines reinforcing continuity across time zones.

By New York, it continues.

Subtle human moments imply awareness and choice: movement through a terminal corridor, a professional pause at a window, traffic streaming below from a rooftop vantage. The city progression should now feel intentional and complete—Tokyo, London, New York—with each moment connected by quiet financial awareness rather than spectacle.

Across time zones. Across lives.

SCENE 04INT./EXT. NEW YORK HIGH-RISE / CITY LIGHTS / ROOFTOP HORIZON - DUSK TO NIGHT

Day folds into evening in New York. Office towers glow, rooftop horizons give way to a dense field of night lights, and restrained Kraken purple begins to appear subtly in reflections, signage edges, and atmospheric highlights. The visual language resolves the global day into a composed close without overpowering the city.

When the moment comes—

TRADER sits in a quiet high-rise interior with the city glowing outside. She calmly reviews her Kraken portfolio on a larger screen or tablet, absorbing information with poise and control. Light trails, elevator reflections, and illuminated windows align with calm decision-making rather than adrenaline, allowing this final character beat to express readiness through confidence.

—be ready.

SCENE 05INT./EXT. BRANDED PURPLE WORLD / GLOBAL CITY ABSTRACT - NIGHT

The city journey resolves into a refined branded environment where the visual memory of Tokyo, London, and New York condenses into one premium global tableau. Real urban textures blend with Kraken's restrained purple palette as architectural lines, horizon lights, and abstract motion settle into a secure, composed brand world. The Kraken logo appears with elegant restraint.

Tokyo. London. New York. When you act, Kraken is ready.

End card holds cleanly with minimal text: Kraken logo, brand name, and a restrained call to action. On-screen text: 'Learn More'. The finish should feel secure, global, and authoritative—never loud.
